01 BIOGRAPHY

Meet Aarav.

Crafting spatial poetry at the intersection of timeless Indian heritage and quiet modern minimalism.

Aarav Mehta entered the world of architectural interiors with a singular conviction: that a residence should never feel like a transient showroom, but an intimate sanctuary deeply anchored in the personality, ancestry, and daily rituals of its inhabitants.

Trained in architectural design and influenced by the rich textural vernacular of Rajasthan alongside refined European minimalism, Aarav spent over a decade and a half honing his craft on high-profile private residences, palatial villas, and boutique luxury estates.

His studio operates with an uncompromising focus on bespoke craftsmanship, sourcing rare Indian marbles, hand-hewn timbers, fluted brass detailing, and curated art pieces. Rather than imposing a repetitive signature aesthetic, Aarav listens to the subtleties of his clients' lives, translating unspoken aspirations into tactile, luminous spaces.

06 DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

"Design Is Not Decoration."

Decoration masks a room; design reveals its soul. Every line drawn by our studio stems from a deep reverence for authentic materials, balanced light, and human scale.

01

Light

Natural illumination is the primary building material. We sculpt openings to capture morning serenity and warm golden hour glows.

02

Material

Honesty in materials. Real stone that ages gracefully, authentic timbers with grain, and hand-finished lime plasters.

03

Texture

Rich tactile contrasts — smooth honed marble against raw bouclé, brushed brass against textured slub linens.

04

Proportion

Golden ratios and intentional voids. We celebrate negative space so hero pieces breathe with regal dignity.

05

Function

Uncompromising ergonomic intelligence. A luxury home must operate effortlessly in every daily human interaction.

06

Craftsmanship

Honoring master Indian generational artisans — stone carvers, brass smiths, and weavers — in high-modern contexts.
